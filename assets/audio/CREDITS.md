# Adhan recordings

Every sound `adhan_<id>.wav` is a real muezzin recording supplied with the app
(source files in `mp3/`), cut to <= 28 s with a 2 s fade-out by
`tool/adhan_audio.py`. The app owner is responsible for holding the right to
redistribute them.
